About Astrakhan State Medical University

Astrakhan State Medical University is one of the oldest medical institutions in Russia, situated in the Volga river delta. It is highly recognized for its clinical research, global student exchanges, and extensive clinical practice.

Key Highlights

  • One of Russia's oldest and most prestigious medical schools, founded in 1918
  • Located in Astrakhan — a beautiful, historical trading city on the Caspian Sea
  • Extensive clinical departments in city-wide hospitals with rich patient contact
  • Globally recognized degree valid for licensing exams (FMGE, NExT, USMLE)
